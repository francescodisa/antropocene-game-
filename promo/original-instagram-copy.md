# Instagram caption

The end of the world has two bosses.

Antropocene is a small satirical beat ’em up set in a ruined city, where Trump and Netanyahu await at the end of the road. Choose a rainbow martial artist, a punk guitarist, a Marxist built like a tank or a hacker, and fight your way through two stages of political absurdity.

Mamdani sometimes lends a hand. Tajani wanders around looking lost and drops bottles of questionable contents. Someone who calls you a comrade may kick you a moment later.

Play in your browser, on desktop or mobile.
